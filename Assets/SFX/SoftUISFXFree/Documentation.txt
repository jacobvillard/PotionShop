Soft UI SFX Free

Documentation. 25 sounds as WAV (44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono), one AudioClip each, a folder
per kind:

  cancel/ (2)
    back_01, cancel_01
  clicks/ (3)
    click_01, click_pop_01, click_wood_01
  confirm/ (3)
    confirm_01, confirm_03, confirm_pluck_01
  drag/ (1)
    drop_01
  error/ (2)
    error_01, error_buzz_01
  hover/ (2)
    hover_01, hover_tick_01
  notification/ (2)
    notify_01, notify_03
  pop/ (2)
    bubble_01, pop_01
  reward/ (2)
    coin, level_up
  swish/ (1)
    swish_01
  toggles/ (2)
    toggle_off_01, toggle_on_01
  typing/ (1)
    type_01
  windows/ (2)
    close_01, open_01

  Demo/SoftUISFXFreeDemo.unity   a button per sound

Play one with AudioSource.PlayOneShot(clip). The WAVs import in any Unity version; the demo
scene needs Unity 6.3 or later.
